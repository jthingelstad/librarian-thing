Amazon makes everything really easy, particularly wish lists, but not everything I would desire for a gift is sold on Amazon and frankly I don't feel a need to send all of our money to Amazon. This page is a "list of lists" for places that I actively keep lists of gifts or items I would like.

For **books** I'm keeping that [list on Bookshop.org](https://bookshop.org/wishlists/632809c63c50ed1a42f156349c301e7c2541c2ad). Bookshop is a great book store that is directly working with local book stores to keep them part of our communities.

For **electronics and gear** I have this [list on B&H Photo Video](https://www.bnh.com/wish/e1dcce63842b831573c5ce80c40d595a/). B&H has everything around cameras and AV gear. 

And yes, like most people I do have a [list on  Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/hz/wishlist/ls/2XZDTON2G5GP4) as well with some things there.
