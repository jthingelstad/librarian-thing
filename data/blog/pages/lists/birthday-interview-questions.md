Each year on their birthdays I interview our kids and ask them the same set of questions. These videos are a great reference to what they thought each year they were growing up.

1. How old are you going to be?
2. What is your favorite color?
3. How tall are you?
4. What is your favorite food?
5. Who are some of your best friends?
6. What is your favorite show?
7. What is your favorite thing to do?
8. What is the best part of your day?
9. Where would you like to go for vacation?
10. What do you want to do when you grow up?
11. What is your favorite movie?
12. What is your favorite animal?
13. If you could be an animal, what would it be?
14. How would you describe yourself?
15. What is your favorite kind of weather?
16. What is your favorite book?
17. What do you want to do this year?
18. What was your favorite thing you did last year?
19. What is your favorite video game?
20. What is your favorite Pokemon?
21. What is your biggest goal in life?
22. How do you like being a big sister?
23. How much is your allowance?
24. What is your favorite sport?
25. Who is your favorite teacher and why? (Start asking in Middle school)

26. Tell a story about something
