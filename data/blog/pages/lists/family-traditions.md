Every family has traditions, a lot of them are traditions that we all share, but we have some that we have created for ourselves.

- **Boat Day:** This is the day that we get the Pontoon out of winter storage and truly begin summer. I get so excited for Boat Day that Tammy and the kids have turned it into a real event, often with gifts and everything!
