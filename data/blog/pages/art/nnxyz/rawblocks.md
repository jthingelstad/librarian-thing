**nnxyz rawblocks** is one of three series of inks that [nnxyz created on NiftyInk](https://nifty.ink/artist/0x9410bc26732ed9480833cb3a4a0f377908e261e2). rawblocks consists of 65 "inks" plus an origin 0000 ink. They were created between Sun, 08 May 2022 23:47:31 GMT and Wed, 15 Mar 2023 05:38:44 GMT. rawblocks #1 and #2 were created while nnxyz was still creating many additional inks in the nnotb collection as well as some nifty faces. The rawblocks collection really took off with rawblocks #3 on Sun, 30 Oct 2022 21:41:56 GMT, over five months after the first two. nnxyz only created rawblocks inks after that. rawblocks is the third and final collection that nnxyz has said they will create on NiftyInk.

By [purchasing](https://blockscout.com/xdai/mainnet/tx/0x94976b55c3247d5037e24532dcf81257a1d9ee7e0bf9dbdfc4325639b674cc49) the only **1 of 1 of rawblocks #0000** [nnxyz.thingelstad.eth owns the only complete rawblocks collection](https://nifty.ink/holdings/0xf872525e4dba6ca8418fe5319b5959de7b947a37). All remaining unsold rawblocks were burned.
