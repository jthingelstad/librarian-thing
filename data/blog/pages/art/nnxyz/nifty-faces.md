**nnxyz nifty faces** is one of three series of inks that [nnxyz created on NiftyInk](https://nifty.ink/artist/0x9410bc26732ed9480833cb3a4a0f377908e261e2). nifty faces consists of 132 inks plus an origin 0000 and xmas special. They were created between Tue, 26 Oct 2021 17:34:51 GMT and Sun, 22 May 2022 15:47:17 GMT. nifty faces is the first of three collections that nnxyz created on NiftyInk.

By [purchasing](https://blockscout.com/xdai/mainnet/tx/0xb0c7b0a25dd427eb78eba7d5a53010e96992d537fe6599b1b2cf678af8af52e1) the only **1 of 1 of nifty faces #0000** [nnxyz.thingelstad.eth owns the only complete rawblocks collection](https://nifty.ink/holdings/0xf872525e4dba6ca8418fe5319b5959de7b947a37). All remaining unsold nifty faces were burned.
