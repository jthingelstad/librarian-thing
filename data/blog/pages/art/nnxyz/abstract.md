**[nnxyz abstract](https://opensea.io/collection/nnxyz-abstract)** is a series of abstract art consisting of 50 pieces plus an origin 0000 and a proof-of-stake. They were minted between March 30 2022 and April 4 2022.

By [purchasing](https://polygonscan.com/tx/0xebc239be2a36df7caf5076b9c1e514f4acf9813c7553920aeb9ec00605055122) the only **1 of 1 of abstract #0000** [nnxyz.thingelstad.eth owns the only complete abstract collection](https://nifty.ink/holdings/0xb009e269060c0d6e652176c166c96c97965ca19f). All remaining unsold abstracts will be burned.

Individual items of this collection were gifted to notable individuals that showed commitment to nnxyz's work on NiftyInk. This collection also represents a network of notable artists and supporters.
