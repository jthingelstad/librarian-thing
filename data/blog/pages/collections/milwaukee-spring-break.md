Some go to beach locations for Spring Break — we decided to go to Milwaukee! Milwaukee may not seem like a typical spring break location but we had a blast. The weather didn’t do us any favors. The unseasonably warm winter decided to get cold and rainy during our trip but we didn’t let that damper our fun. The trip was built around the Brett Goldstein show as well as getting to the Flight Simulator just south of Milwaukee. [Saint Kate](https://www.saintkatearts.com) was a fabulous home base for our time there.

As we departed for home everyone attested that they had a fabulous time. 🤩
