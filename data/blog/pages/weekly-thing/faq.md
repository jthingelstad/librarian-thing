### When is the Weekly Thing published?

The Weekly Thing is sent on Saturday mornings at 7:00am central time.

### Are there breaks?

I take a summer and winter break from publishing the Weekly Thing. In the summer, that break is from July 1 to August 31. In the winter it is December 15 to January 14. There is no issue on Saturday during those breaks. This is a great way for me to take a break and recharge. I often use it to make technical improvements to my automation as well.
