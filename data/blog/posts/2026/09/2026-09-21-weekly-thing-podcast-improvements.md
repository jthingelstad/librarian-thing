Claude and I did a session in [WT Builder](https://github.com/jthingelstad/wt-builder) focused on the generated audio for [Weekly Thing](https://weekly.thingelstad.com) issues. This was one of the goals I had with the new authoring app.

- Audio pauses will now fall in natural breaks in the flow. Each functional block of text is generated on its own.
- Better section announcements and handoffs.
- Blockquotes "quote" and "end quote" again.
- Lists are spoken better.
- I'm now embedding images from the issue into the metadata. If your podcast player supports that you will see them as the podcast plays.
- Better pronunciation.
- The descriptions now contain the links, so for the first time ever you can actually get to the articles from the podcast version.

With these changes the [podcast version](https://weekly.thingelstad.com/podcast/) really can be a viable way to engage with the Weekly Thing.
