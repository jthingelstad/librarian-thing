My cousin Josh has been writing his [Rambling Josh](https://www.ramblingjosh.com) column for over 20 years. (Short aside, it is funny to me that I've been blogging for 20 years as well. His column and my blog are very close to the same age.) In 2024 I [helped Josh get all of his writing on the web](https://www.thingelstad.com/2024/06/30/rambling-josh.html) so that it is preserved and accessible to the world. I'm super happy that everyone can now read his writing. And now, he's created a book from his columns. He sent me a copy (signed and all!) and it looks amazing. Go [buy a copy](https://shop.ingramspark.com/b/084). You can also [read Six and A Lot about it](https://www.ramblingjosh.com/2026/09/02/six-and-a-lot.html). Congrats cousin!
