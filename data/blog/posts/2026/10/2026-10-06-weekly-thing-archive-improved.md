Over the 10 years that I've published the Weekly Thing I have used three different platforms to send it. Each one of those had unique requirements for how I structured the issues. For a while I've wanted to get the entire archive updated so that no matter how far you go back the issues have the similar structure that they have today. With the help of Claude I was able to make this happen! The [full archive](https://weekly.thingelstad.com/archive/) is now updated renders cleanly. It required pretty extensive source doctoring behind the scenes.

Along with that I was able to then render every issue in audio as well. Now the Weekly Thing Podcast feed goes all the way back to the very first issue as well which is pretty cool.
