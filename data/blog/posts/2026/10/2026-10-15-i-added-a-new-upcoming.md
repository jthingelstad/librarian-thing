I added a new Upcoming feature to [Release Notes](https://notes.yourversionnumber.com):

> Release Notes now has a new section for what's coming next. In Upcoming you can save birthdays, anniversaries, trips, and one-time events. You can choose when you would like these events to show up in your Release Notes. Trips include a destination with a start and end date. All of these upcoming events are private to only you.
